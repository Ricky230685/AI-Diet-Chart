About AI Diet Chart Planner

Helping people achieve their health goals through personalized nutrition.

Our Mission

AI Diet Chart Planner is designed to provide personalized diet recommendations based on an individual's age, weight, height, lifestyle, and fitness goals.

Whether your goal is weight loss, weight gain, or maintaining a healthy lifestyle, our platform generates customized meal plans to support your journey.

Our aim is to make healthy eating simple, accessible, and data-driven for everyone.

What We Offer

🤖 AI Recommendations

Smart diet suggestions based on user information.

⚖ BMI Calculator

Calculate BMI and understand your health status.

🥗 Personalized Diet Plans

Meal plans tailored to your fitness goals.

Project Information

This project is developed using Django, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and Python.

The goal is to combine technology and nutrition to help users make healthier lifestyle choices.
